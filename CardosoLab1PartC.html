Pozole Rojo Recipe

This are the Ingredients needed for the pozole.

  • 2 lbs pork and cut into chunks
  • 1 large can of white hominy, drain and rinsed
  • 5 dried guajillo chiles
  • 2 dired ancho chiles
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 medium onion
  • Salt to taste
  • Chicken broth
  • Toppings: shredded cabbage, radished, lime wedges, oregano

Steps to Prepare

  1. Remove seeds and stems from the dried chiles, then soak them in how water.
  2. Blend the softened chiles with garlic, onion, and some of the soaking liquid to make the red sauce.
  3. In a large pot, cook the pork in water or broth until it tenders. This should take about 1-2 hours.
  4. Add the red chile sauce to the pot, strring well to combine.
  5. Add the hominy and simmer for another 30 minutes, allowing the flavor to blend.
  6. Serve hot with shredded cabbage, sliced radished, oregano, and lime wedges on the top.
